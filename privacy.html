Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The Black Bird Society (contact: contact@theblackbirdsociety.com) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data Black Bird Training collects, why, and your rights.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your name and email address, or the basic profile shared when you sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Training data: your plan answers (goal, level, days, place), workouts completed, weights, ranks, member number and settings.
  • Purchase data: subscription status. Payment details are handled by Paddle.com; we never see or store your card number.
  • Progress data (optional backup): streak, XP, badges, body stats (weight, height, goal) and progress photos. If "Back up to my account" is on, these are stored privately in your account so they follow you to a new phone. Turn it off and they stay on your phone only.
  • Technical data: basic device and usage information needed to run the App, and data stored on your device to remember your settings.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the App: your plan, progress, ranks and member card.
  • To manage your subscription and support requests.
  • To improve the App and keep it secure.
  • To show ads in the free version (see section 4).

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

  • Paddle.com – payments, taxes and invoices (Merchant of Record).
  • Supabase – secure storage of accounts and training data.
  • GitHub Pages – website hosting.
  • Google AdSense – ads in the free version, which may use cookies.

These providers only process data to deliver their service to us. Some may be located outside your country; they use recognised safeguards to protect your data.

4. Cookies and ads

The App uses local storage to remember your settings. On our website and in the free version of the App, our ad partner Google (AdSense) may use cookies to show and measure ads. You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. Premium members do not see ads. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

5. Health-related data and photos

Training data such as weights and workouts is used only to run your plan and track your progress. Pregnancy mode and health conditions you select in the App are saved on your phone only and are never sent to us. Progress photos are private by default: only you can see them unless you choose to share a photo with your gym partners or your team, and you can make it private again or delete it at any time.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except what we must keep by law (for example, purchase records kept by Paddle).

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or limit certain uses. Most changes can be made directly in the App; for anything else, email contact@theblackbirdsociety.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you about important changes.
