Why am I so sore?

Recovery1 min readThe Black Bird Society

That stiff feeling a day or two after training is called DOMS. It's normal, often peaks 24 to 72 hours after, and is not caused by lactic acid.

It's strongest after new exercises or a big jump in effort, and it gets much milder as your body adapts. Being sore is not proof of a good workout.

Light movement, a walk, sleep, food and water help it pass. Sharp pain, swelling or pain that gets worse is different: stop and get it checked.

General information only, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan, especially if you are pregnant, have a health condition or an injury. Read our health disclaimer.
