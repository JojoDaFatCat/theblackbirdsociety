Machine taken? Swap smart, keep your gains

Training2 min readThe Black Bird Society

Your muscles don't know which machine you use. They only feel tension, range of motion and effort. If your machine is taken, pick a move that trains the same muscle along a similar path.

Keep the same sets and reps. Adjust the weight so the last 2 reps are hard but clean. Free weights feel heavier than machines, so start a little lighter.

The app does this for you: tap "Machine taken?" and it shows moves for the same muscles, with the weight already converted.
