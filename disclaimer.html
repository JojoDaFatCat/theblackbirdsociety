Health & fitness disclaimer

The content on this website and in the Black Bird Training app, including workouts, meal ideas, articles and answers from Muninn, is for general information and education only. It is not medical advice and does not replace advice from a doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian or other qualified professional.

Talk to your doctor before you start any new exercise or nutrition plan, especially if you are pregnant or recently gave birth, are over 60, have a heart, lung, joint or other health condition, take medication, or have an injury.

Stop exercising and get medical help right away if you feel chest pain, severe shortness of breath, dizziness, faintness or unusual pain.
